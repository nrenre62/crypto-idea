Privacy Policy

How CryptoIdea handles your data.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

CryptoIdea ("CryptoIdea", "we", "us") is an open-source crypto portfolio tracker and research tool. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are. This service is operated by [OPERATOR NAME], based in [JURISDICTION]. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account. Your email address and a password, handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). We never see your password.
  • Your content. The portfolios, holdings, transactions, investment notes ("theses"), and learning progress you create. This is stored privately per account and is not visible to other users.
  • Subscription (paid plans only). Payments are processed by PayPal. We store only your plan tier and a subscription reference — never your card or bank details.
  • Diagnostics. To keep the service running we collect error reports, performance traces, and session replays through Sentry (see below).
  • Analytics (optional). If enabled, aggregate usage analytics via Google Analytics and/or Plausible.

Market data shown in the app comes from CoinGecko through our own server proxy; no personal data about you is sent to CoinGecko.

Error monitoring & session replay (Sentry)

We use Sentry to detect crashes and measure performance so the product stays live. This includes session replay, which records a masked reconstruction of your browser session (interactions and page structure) for a sample of sessions and when errors occur. Replay masks all text and blocks all media by default, and we strip identifying data from error reports. Sentry data is processed in the European Union (Germany) — a transfer to a third-party processor.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the service — performing our agreement with you (your account and content).
  • To keep it secure and reliable — our legitimate interest (error monitoring, abuse prevention, audit records).
  • Analytics and replay cookies — your consent, where required.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that run CryptoIdea: Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting), PayPal (payments), Sentry (diagnostics), and, if enabled, Google Analytics / Plausible (analytics) and Termly (consent management). Each processes data on our behalf under its own terms.

How long we keep it

  • Account & content — until you delete your account.
  • Deleted accounts — moved to a 30-day recovery "trash", then permanently erased.
  • Security/audit records — up to 12 months (see below).

Your rights

You can access, export, correct, or delete your data at any time from Account → Privacy & your data in the app (export as JSON/CSV; delete with a 30-day recovery window). Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. To make any request, contact [CONTACT EMAIL].

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, isolated per account by server-side security rules, and secrets are held server-side only. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we design for least privilege and minimum data.

Children

CryptoIdea is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide a notice in the app.

CryptoIdea is an open-source (MIT-licensed) research and portfolio project, provided for informational purposes only. It is not investment, financial, or tax advice.

Security & audit records: administrative and account-security events (for example account deletion, data export, or subscription changes) are kept in a security audit log for up to 12 months on the basis of our legitimate interest in protecting the service, then deleted automatically. These records are retained for the full period even if the related account is deleted earlier.
