## Starter — free

For people checking out crypto for the first time, or holding a small number of coins they don't want to micro-manage.

You get:

- Three portfolios, up to 30 coins each, 300 transactions per coin.
- Live prices and real profit-and-loss tracking. Same prices, same speed as Premium.
- Journal before you buy — record your thesis on each coin so future-you can review it honestly.
- Conviction signals on every coin you hold, refreshed on a shared cache.
- Portfolio Pulse and Ask in data-driven summary mode — insights generated from your own data rather than a live AI call. Fast, free, accurate.
- Two Learn modules to get you grounded in how to research, not just price.
- Privacy by default — your portfolio is yours, and it is never sold.

What's not included: live AI on Pulse and Ask. You see the data-driven summary instead. Upgrading unlocks the live model.

## Pro — $9.99/month or $99.99/year (2 months free)

For investors who take their research seriously and want the live AI layer without committing to Premium yet.

You get everything in Starter, plus:

- 6 portfolios, 100 coins each, 1,000 transactions per coin.
- Live AI on Pulse and Ask — a generous daily allowance. Ask "what changed in my portfolio this week" or "is my thesis still intact" and get a live, model-generated answer that reads your journal.
- Five Learn modules unlocked (research framework, value-investing principles, position sizing, exit planning, common traps).
- Priority cache — your held coins get refreshed sooner in the shared conviction cache.
- Standard support.

Annual billing is $99.99/year ($8.33/mo equivalent) — two full months versus paying monthly.

## Premium — $49.99/month or $499.99/year (2 months free)

For serious portfolios with serious ambitions — multiple positions, multiple narratives, real research workflows.

You get everything in Pro, plus:

- 15 portfolios, up to 200 coins each, 2,000 transactions per coin.
- Live AI on Pulse, Ask, and conviction — the highest daily allowance, enough headroom to use it as a research partner across every position, every week.
- Unlimited journaling and thesis history — review and amend conviction notes without size limits.
- Priority Pulse — your portfolio gets fresher AI summaries faster.
- All nine Learn modules unlocked, covering the full research craft.
- Priority support.

Annual billing is $499.99/year ($41.67/mo equivalent) — best value for multi-portfolio research, both the savings and the headroom to use it.

## How the AI limits work

Live AI calls cost money to run. To keep the price flat and predictable for you, each paid plan includes a monthly allowance:

- Pro: a generous daily allowance on average.
- Premium: a much larger daily allowance on average.

If you reach the limit, the app smoothly switches to the data-driven summaries — the same ones Starter sees — for the rest of the month, with a reminder that your live allowance resets at the start of the next month. Nothing breaks. No surprise charges. No paywall in the middle of a thought.

Heavy day? You're covered — the daily figures are averages, not hard caps. The real ceiling is a monthly budget, and most users finish the month with headroom to spare.

## What conviction signals means

Every coin in your portfolio gets a small set of objective signals about the project, refreshed automatically:

- Development activity — is the project actively shipping code?
- Founder presence — has the team appeared publicly in the last quarter?
- Community signal — is engagement growing, flat, or fading?
- Liquidity and holders — is the float concentrated or distributed?

Each signal is generated once per coin, then shared across every user holding that coin. You see them on the Research tab next to your holdings. They are not advice. They are the same signals a careful researcher would compile manually — just always there, always current.

## Privacy & ownership

- Your portfolio is yours. It is never sold or shared. It powers features for you only.
- Your journal stays private. Theses are never published or used for anyone else's analysis.
- Export anytime. A one-click CSV or JSON download of everything you've recorded, on every tier including Starter.
- Delete anytime. A 30-day grace period in case you change your mind, then permanent erasure.

## How to choose

- Try Starter first. No card. It's enough to understand what the app does.
- Upgrade to Pro when you start journaling more than a few coins and want the live AI to read what you wrote.
- Upgrade to Premium when you're running multiple portfolios (long-term versus trading, family portfolios, themed baskets) or when the Pro allowance stops feeling like enough.

You can switch tiers anytime. Downgrading trims to the new tier's limits; nothing gets deleted on your side until you say so.
