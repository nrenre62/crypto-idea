## Starter — $0 / free forever

**For:** people checking out crypto for the first time, or holding a small
number of coins they don't want to micro-manage.

**You get:**
- **One portfolio**, up to 10 coins, 50 transactions per coin.
- **Live prices** and real P/L tracking. Same prices, same speed as Premium.
- **Journal before you buy** — record your thesis on each coin so future-you
  can review it honestly.
- **Conviction signals** on every coin you hold (data-driven, refreshed on a
  shared cache).
- **Portfolio Pulse & Ask AI** in *offline summary mode* — same insights,
  generated from your data rather than a live AI call. Fast, free, accurate.
- **2 Learn modules** to get you grounded in how to research, not just price.
- **Privacy by default** — your portfolio is yours. We don't sell data, ever.

**What's not included:** live AI on Pulse / Ask. You'll see the data-driven
fallback instead. Upgrade unlocks the live model.

## How the AI limits work

Live AI calls cost money to run. To keep the price flat and predictable
*for you*, each plan includes a monthly allowance:

- **Pro:** about 13 analyses per day on average.
- **Premium:** about 80 analyses per day on average.

If you reach the limit, the app smoothly switches to **offline data-driven
summaries** — the same ones Starter sees — for the rest of the month, with a
reminder that your live allowance resets on the 1st. **Nothing breaks. No
surprise charges. No paywall in the middle of a thought.**

Heavy day? You're covered — the daily numbers above are averages, not hard
caps. The real ceiling is a monthly budget, and most users finish the month
with headroom to spare.

## What "conviction signals" means

Every coin on your portfolio gets a small set of objective signals about the
project, refreshed automatically:

- **Development activity** — is the project actively shipping code?
- **Founder presence** — has the team appeared publicly in the last quarter?
- **Community signal** — is engagement growing, flat, or fading?
- **Liquidity & holders** — is the float concentrated or distributed?

Each signal is generated **once per coin**, then shared across every user
holding that coin. You see them on the **Research** tab next to your
holdings. They are not advice. They're the same signals a careful researcher
would compile manually — just always there, always current.

## Privacy & ownership

- **Your portfolio is yours.** We don't sell it. We don't share it. We use it
  to power features for you only.
- **Your journal stays private.** We never publish theses or use them for
  anyone else's analysis.
- **Export anytime.** A one-click CSV or JSON download of everything you've
  recorded — available on every tier, including Starter.
- **Delete anytime.** A 30-day grace period in case you change your mind,
  then permanent erasure.

## How to choose

- **Try Starter first.** No card. It's enough to understand what we do.
- **Upgrade to Pro** when you start journaling more than a few coins and want
  the live AI to actually read what you wrote.
- **Upgrade to Premium** when you're running multiple portfolios (long-term
  vs. trading, family member portfolios, themed baskets) or when 13
  analyses/day stops feeling like enough.

You can switch tiers anytime. Downgrading trims to the new tier's limits;
nothing gets deleted on your side until you say so.
